About Us

Who We Are

Founded in 2001, the University College London Investment Society is UCL's leading finance society, built on a mission to equip students with the skills, knowledge and experience needed to pursue successful careers in finance.

Today, UCLIS is one of the largest societies on campus, with a community of over 1,200 ambitious members from more than 50 degree backgrounds. Our diverse membership reflects our belief that finance should be accessible to students from all academic disciplines.

We support our members through development programmes, senior speaker events, technical workshops, mentorship opportunities and real-world investing experience. Top students also have the opportunity to apply to manage UCLIS' £10,000 real-money investment fund.

From our flagship Finance Conference to our fund management programme, UCLIS provides a platform for students to learn, connect and grow within the finance industry.
